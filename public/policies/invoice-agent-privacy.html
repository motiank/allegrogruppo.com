Allegro Invoice Agent – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Allegro Invoice Agent ("the app") is an internal bookkeeping tool of Allegro Gruppo Ltd (אלגרו גרופו בע"מ) and Pasta Lina Ltd (פסטה לינה בע"מ) ("we"). It is used only with our own company accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app does

The app looks in our company mailbox for invoices, reads the invoice details, and saves each invoice file in a folder in the Google Drive of the same account.

Google user data the app uses

  • Gmail (read only): emails that match an invoice search, including sender, recipient, subject, date, text, links and attachments. The app cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Drive (only files the app created): the app creates folders, invoice files and index files. It cannot see any other file in Drive.
  • Email address of the account: used to show which account is connected.

How we use this data

We use it only to find invoices and file them for our bookkeeping. From each invoice the app records the issuer, the customer, the invoice number, the total, the date and the Israeli Tax Authority allocation number (מספר הקצאה), if there is one. We do not use the data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who receives the data

To read an email and its files, the app sends them to Claude, an AI service of Anthropic, which works as our service provider for this task. We share the data with no other party, unless the law requires it. Invoices saved in Drive stay in our Google account.

We do not use Google user data to train or improve general AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Allegro Invoice Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The login token and a list of the emails already read (email id, sender, subject and result) are kept on a computer that we control, with access limited to us. Data moves to Google and Anthropic over encrypted connections.

How long we keep data

We keep invoices and their details for as long as our bookkeeping and the law require. The app works on an email and its files in memory and does not save them on the computer. Only the invoice files are saved, in Google Drive.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time on the Google Account permissions page. To ask us to delete data, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
